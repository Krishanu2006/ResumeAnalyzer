# Resume Analyzer — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

**Version:** 2.0  
**Status:** Planned  
**Product:** Resume Analyzer  
**Primary Goal:** Transform Resume Analyzer from an AI-generated report tool into a personalized interview preparation platform.

---

## 1. Product Overview

Resume Analyzer is an AI-powered career preparation platform that analyzes a candidate's resume against a target Job Description (JD).

### Current V1 Features

- Resume–JD Match Score
- Skill Gap Analysis
- Technical Interview Questions
- Behavioral Interview Questions
- Preparation Roadmap
- Resume PDF Download
- Saved Reports
- Previous Reports
- Guest Report Generation
- Authentication

### V2 Goal

Version 2 will move beyond static report generation.

Instead of only telling users:

> "How well does my resume match this job?"

V2 should help users answer:

> "What should I learn, practice, and improve to actually become ready for this job?"

The product will evolve into an **AI-powered Interview Preparation Platform**.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Candidates often struggle to understand:

1. Whether their resume matches a job.
2. Which skills they are missing.
3. Which interview questions they are likely to face.
4. How they should prepare.
5. Whether their resume is ATS-friendly.
6. How they are performing in interviews.
7. Whether their preparation is improving over time.

V1 solves the first few problems.

V2 should connect the entire journey:

```text
Resume
   ↓
Job Description
   ↓
Resume Analysis
   ↓
Skill Gaps
   ↓
Personalized Preparation
   ↓
Practice
   ↓
Mock Interview
   ↓
Performance Analysis
   ↓
Improvement
